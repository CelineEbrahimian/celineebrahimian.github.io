Electrium Mobility: Electric Motorcycle

Mechanical subteam
FMEAStatic FEASolidWorksEnclosure design

What

Converting a gas Kawasaki motorcycle the team bought into an electric motorcycle. My focus: structural analysis and the dashboard.

How

  • Led FMEA on the motorcycle's subsystems
  • Ran static FEA on the steel frame, raising safety factors to cover dynamic loads
  • Re-modelled the stock dashboard in SolidWorks from hand measurements
  • Designed a waterproof enclosure for a new PCB-based digital display
  • Iterated CAD for the dashboard and motor-wheel assemblies

Impact

  • Flagged a suspension mounting bracket for reinforcement before it could fail in fatigue
  • Waterproof dashboard with tighter packaging and better data access
